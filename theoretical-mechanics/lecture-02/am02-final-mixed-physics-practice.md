## AM02-X01

**A movable wedge and two conservation laws** — Derivation and transfer; 25–40 min.

Prerequisites: AM02 Unit 02-4 - Cyclic Coordinates and Generalized Momentum (not included; [edition scope](../../SOURCES.md#pending)) · [AM02 Math B - Homogeneity and Kinetic Forms](../../mathematics/lecture-02/am02-math-b-homogeneity-and-kinetic-forms.md).

A block of mass $m$ slides without friction on a wedge of mass $M$ moving frictionlessly on a horizontal floor. The face rises rightward at angle $0<\beta<\pi/2$. Let $X$ locate the wedge and let $s$ increase downhill: block position is $(X-s\cos\beta,h-s\sin\beta)$. Released from rest, derive both accelerations, the horizontal conserved momentum, and the normal force. State the contact condition.
