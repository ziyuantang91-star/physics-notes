## AM02-F01

[Return to question](am02-final-focused-physics-practice.md#section-2).

> **Hint 1**
> Write the torque equation before solving for angular velocity. The actuator has no torque.

> **Hint 2**
> Let $\ell=mr^2\dot\theta$. It obeys a constant-coefficient first-order equation even though $r$ varies.

> **Hint 3**
> Use $\dot\ell+(b/m)\ell=\tau_0$; then use the radial equation with $\ddot r=0$.

## AM02-F02

[Return to question](am02-final-focused-physics-practice.md#section-3).

> **Hint 1**
> At release the squared-speed terms vanish, but acceleration coupling remains.

> **Hint 2**
