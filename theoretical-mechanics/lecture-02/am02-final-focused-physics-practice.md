## AM02-F01

**Torque, drag and a prescribed radius** — Derivation and transfer; 25–40 min.

Prerequisites: AM02 Unit 02-2 - Generalized Forces in Polar Coordinates (not included; [edition scope](../../SOURCES.md#pending)) · [AM02 Math A - Moving Bases and Vector Products](../../mathematics/lecture-02/am02-math-a-moving-bases-and-vector-products.md).

A bead of mass $m$ moves in an inertial horizontal plane with $r(t)=r_0+ut>0$, enforced by a radial actuator. It experiences isotropic drag $-b\mathbf v$ and a counterclockwise torque $\tau_0$, realized by tangential force $\tau_0/r$. Given $\dot\theta(0)=\omega_0$, find angular momentum, angular speed, the actuator's radial force and its power. Take $b>0$.
