> **Hint 1**
> The radial and angular velocity components are $\dot R$ and $R\dot\theta$.

> **Hint 2**

## AM01-M07

[Return to problem](am01-mixed-physics-practice.md#section-9)

> **Hint 1**
> The squared speed is $R^2(\dot\theta^2+\Omega^2\sin^2\theta)$.

> **Hint 2**
> Write the reduced equation as $mR^2\ddot\theta=-dV_{\rm eff}/d\theta$ with $V_{\rm eff}=-mR^2\Omega^2\sin^2\theta/2-mgR\cos\theta$.

> **Hint 3**
> At the critical value the quadratic term at the bottom vanishes; retain the next nonzero Taylor term. For power, compare physical energy with the conserved reduced energy.
