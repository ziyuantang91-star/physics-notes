# Track A — Scientific Literature and Review Writing

> **Edition scope:** original AI-assisted supplement or study material. Some prerequisite lessons are withheld for source review; their titles are plain text, not unavailable links. [Attribution and scope](../SOURCES.md#pending).


> **Review status: framework.** Educational framework; this is not a record of learner progress. [Sources and limitations](../SOURCES.md).


[Current dashboard](README.md) · [Detailed Lesson 1](review-writing-lesson-1-from-question-to-evidence.md) · [Independent assignment](review-writing-assignment-1.md). These extend this existing track; no study completion is inferred.

| Stage | Practice | Measurable output | Self-assessment before advancing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Paper anatomy | Identify question, methods, results, discussion, and limits in one accessible paper | One annotated paper note | Can I locate the evidence behind the abstract's main claim? |
| 2. Critical reading and source verification | Check identifier, version, figures, conditions, and access gaps | Verified bibliographic record plus three located extractions | Can another reader find each extracted statement? |
| 3. Evidence extraction | Separate reported results, author mechanisms, and my inference | Three evidence records with uncertainty and limitations | Did I avoid attributing mechanism from observation alone? |
| 4. Literature synthesis | Compare a small, deliberately chosen set of relevant sources | One comparison table including a conflict or explicit unresolved gap | Are definitions and conditions compatible? |
| 5. Argument development | Form a bounded claim and test alternatives | One claim-to-evidence map | What would weaken or overturn my argument? |
| 6. Scientific writing and referencing | Draft an evidence-linked paragraph with precise attribution | A short synthesis passage and source checklist | Does every factual assertion have traceable support? |
| 7. Peer-review-style revision | Review evidence, logic, scope, figures, and reproducibility | Reviewer comments, response log, and revised passage | Can I explain each revision without relying on an AI answer? |

Use [Track D](../latex/latex-track.md) to typeset small pieces of your own review work. Keep claim-to-source traceability, limitations, and verification flags when transferring content; formatting success does not establish scientific validity. The LaTeX exercises complement this track without rewriting the full review or restarting its calendar.

Do not expand the literature broadly just to fill a table. Choose accessible sources already relevant to the project, and state what is unavailable.
